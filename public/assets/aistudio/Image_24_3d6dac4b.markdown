## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, cinematic aesthetic tailored for enterprise environmental intelligence, industrial water stewardship, and IoT conservation analytics. It targets chief sustainability officers, facility engineers, and municipal resource planners who require mission-critical real-time metrics presented with the clarity and poise of an aerospace control room.

The aesthetic fuses deep-ocean darkness with bioluminescent data visualization. Rather than relying on standard corporate eco-green palettes, the interface anchors in abyssal oceanic tones: deep midnight navy and lightless obsidian surfaces punctuated by crisp electric blues and radiant cyan telemetry. 

The design combines glassmorphism with high-precision technical layouts:
- **Translucent Obsidian Slabs:** Multi-tiered glass panels with low-opacity dark tints and backdrop blurs to preserve depth across dense data views.
- **Precision Luminescence:** Focused light emissions, hairline 1px illuminated borders, and directional gradients mimicking light refracted through water.
- **Micro-Data Density:** Instrument-grade indicators, monospace data values, and fluid charts that convey authority, real-time fidelity, and uncompromising environmental stewardship.

## Colors

The color palette is built for a pure dark-mode runtime environment. It leverages low-wavelength navy tones as the structural substrate, directing user attention toward radiant, luminescent water-index indicators.

### Key Roles & Values
- **Canvas Base (`#060B14`):** The foundational substrate. An uncompromising, abyssal midnight black-blue.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0B132B`):** Base card backgrounds and container tiles.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#0E1B38`):** Raised telemetry modules, active states, and floating navigation bars.
- **Primary Accent (`#00F0FF`):** Radiant cyan. Reserved for key numerical metrics, primary actions, active telemetry pulses, and status peaks.
- **Secondary Accent (`#0077B6`):** Focused ocean blue. Used for standard interaction states, data chart primary strokes, and secondary accents.
- **Aquatic Tertiary (`#0D9488`):** Deep oceanic teal. Applied to positive conservation efficiency states, filtration status metrics, and stable trends.
- **Warning / Critical:** An amber-gold (`#F59E0B`) for threshold warnings and a high-voltage coral (`#EF4444`) for critical leak alerts or contamination events.

### Contrast & Balance
Backgrounds must never use neutral greys; all neutrals are tinted with cold midnight blue to maintain an atmospheric visual identity. High-contrast white (`#F8FAFC`) is used for primary headers and key telemetry readouts, while structural subtext is rendered in cool slate-cyan (`#94A3B8`).

## Typography

The typography system pairs geometric forward-leaning structural headings with an ultra-clean body face and monospaced numerical instrumentation.

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Gives primary headers, page titles, and score overviews a sharp, modern technological tone. The geometric glyphs resonate with hydro-architectural flow charts and engineering schematics.
- **Body & Controls (Hanken Grotesk):** Delivers clean legibility on dark glass backgrounds. It avoids the clinical monotony of standard system fonts while maintaining optical balance across complex multi-column telemetry tables.
- **Data & Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Mandated for all sensory metrics, flow rates (GPM / L/min), pH balances, pressure indicators, and timestamps. Tabular layout properties prevent UI jitter during live streaming telemetry updates.

All micro-labels, categorization tags, and system indicators use uppercase `label-caps` with wide letter-spacing (`0.12em`) to mirror environmental field instrumentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid configured to support dense data displays alongside cinematic overview dashboards.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1440px and above):** 12-column grid, `margin-desktop` (3rem), `gutter-lg` (2rem). Content max-width constrained to 1680px for widescreen control rooms.
- **Laptop / Tablet Landscape (1024px - 1439px):** 12-column grid, `margin` (2rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Primary control sidebars collapse into a compact glass rail.
- **Tablet Portrait (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `margin` (1.5rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Telemetry modules reflow into 4-column pairing cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin-mobile` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Telemetry rows switch to full-width horizontal-swipe stacks or linear metric cards.

### Spacing Cadence
Spacing follows an 8-point base rhythm. Tight micro-gaps (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) bind monospaced sensor values to their respective unit descriptors and sparkline charts. Macro-gaps (`space-lg`, `space-xl`, `space-2xl`) separate analytical functional clusters, preventing cognitive fatigue in dark UI dashboards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through calibrated glass layering and luminous edge diffraction rather than standard drop shadows. Heavy, dark drop shadows disappear on `#060B14`, so depth relies on luminous contrast and translucency.

### The Glass Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Ground Level):** `#060B14` tinted with subtle radial gradients of `#0E1B38` fading in from top borders to suggest ambient depth.
2. **Glass Level 1 (Base Container / Grid Cards):** Surface `#0B132B` at 65% opacity, paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a continuous 1px outline of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
3. **Glass Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules / Flyouts):** Surface `#0E1B38` at 80% opacity, paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and a top-edge illuminated border gradient running from `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
4. **Glass Level 3 (Modals / Alert Panels):** Surface `#0E1B38` at 95% opacity with an atmospheric outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 40px -10px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`.

### Light Borders & Glowing Edges
Cards feature asymmetric border highlights: a 1px border where the top and left edges receive a slight cyan-tinted highlight (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.2)`), while bottom and right edges fade to deep translucent blue (`rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.4)`), mimicking an overhead directional oceanic light source.

## Shapes

The design system uses a calibrated medium curvature (Level 2). This provides a polished feel while maintaining clean, technical alignment across telemetry-heavy dashboards.

- **Standard Cards & Containers:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px). Balances high-tech architectural precision with modern elegance.
- **Nested Inner Modules & Metric Wells:** `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px). Used inside parent cards to create structured internal grouping.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px). Maintains clear, tactile click targets.
- **Pills, Status Nodes & Badges:** Fully circular (`rounded-full` / 9999px). Reserved exclusively for status indicators (e.g., active sensor nodes, conservation compliance badges, live alerts).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Radiant Pulse):** Background of linear gradient (135deg, `#00F0FF` to `#0077B6`), text in `#060B14` (semibold `Hanken Grotesk`), rounded-md (8px). Hover state triggers an expanded ambient cyan glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Submerged Glass):** Background of `rgba(14, 27, 56, 0.7)` with a 1px border in `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.3)`. Text in `#F8FAFC`. Hover brings border to full `#00F0FF` and text shifts to `#00F0FF`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `#94A3B8` text, transitioning on hover to `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08)` fill and `#F8FAFC` text.

### Telemetry & Water Score Cards
Cards feature a semi-translucent backdrop (`#0B132B` at 70%) with a 1px top-highlighted border. The card header hosts the category in `label-caps` (`#94A3B8`) accompanied by a 6px status LED. The central body features a large monospaced metric (`telemetry-value-lg`) in pure white or radiant cyan, paired with an inline trend vector and an interactive SVG area sparkline.

### Chips & Badges
- **Live Stream Indicator:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally. Background `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.1)`, 1px border in `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.3)`, text in `label-caps` (`#00F0FF`). Contains a pulsating green or cyan 4px LED dot on the leading edge.
- **Alert Badge:** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, border `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.35)`, text in `#EF4444`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, background `rgba(6, 11, 20, 0.6)`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`. Font set to `Hanken Grotesk` (14px).
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#00F0FF`, with an ambient ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #00F0FF, 0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`. Outline is disabled.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 18px × 18px.
- Unchecked: 1px border in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.3)` over `rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.8)`.
- Checked: Fill `#00F0FF` with icon/dot in `#060B14`. An outer glow ring (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.3)`) signals active state.

### Specialized Water Components
- **Hydro-Gauge Score Dial:** A circular progress arc rendered in radiant cyan-to-teal gradient against a dark track (`#0E1B38`). The center hosts the overall Water Score (0–100) using `display-hero` typography.
- **Acoustic Leak Flow List:** Dense rows alternating in subtle translucent shades, featuring timestamps in `JetBrains Mono`, valve node identifiers, and real-time consumption deltas highlighted in warning amber or conservation teal.
