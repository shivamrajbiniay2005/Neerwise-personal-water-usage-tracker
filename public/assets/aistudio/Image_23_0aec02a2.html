Methodology & Algorithmic Architecture

How the Neerwise Water Score Works

The Neerwise Water Score is an open-standard composite index graded from 0 to 100, harmonizing real-time intake dynamics against climatic baselines.

FACTOR 01 // WEIGHT: 40%

Baseline Climatic Volumetrics

Anchored to household occupant density and ambient evapotranspiration figures. Calibrated strictly against the WHO 135 L/person/day standard adjusted for dynamic seasonal shifts.

B_Factor = V_actual / (N * WHO_ref * ETo) calculate
FACTOR 02 // WEIGHT: 35%

Flow Consistency & Crest Factor

Penalizes erratic burst draws and unmetered continuous flow spikes. Rewards uniform distribution patterns and measured low-flow fixture utilization across daily diurnal cycles.

C_Index = (Q_peak - Q_mean) / Q_sigma stacked_line_chart
FACTOR 03 // WEIGHT: 25%

Micro-Leak Rectification Speed

Measures velocity of resolution when acoustic harmonic patterns detect flapper leaks, irrigation pinholes, or slow valve seepage down to 0.5 Liters per hour.

R_Score = (T_detect - T_repaired) * Lambda timer

Community Benchmark & Transparency Statement

The Neerwise Water Score is an independent peer-to-peer benchmarking index and private conservation framework. It is not an official regulatory municipal certification nor does it supersede certified volumetric municipal revenue meters. Its mathematical models exist solely to empower voluntary behavioral stewardship and community rebate qualification.

The 7-Day Calibration Protocol

Nodes with under 168 hours of continuous telemetry display an active "Calibration Matrix" state. To prevent biased benchmarks, scores are withheld until diurnal baseline variance achieves a 95% statistical confidence interval.
